# Beat map: what the speech does → which device

Read the transcript phrase by phrase. Pick by what the phrase **does**, not by its topic.

| The phrase… | Device | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| any phrase | **stack** with one accent key word | default; always on |
| states a number, price, range, percentage | **figure bar** inside the stack | the unit becomes the small tag |
| makes the big claim about the viewer ("you are…", "this is why…") | **headline** | ≤ 1 per 15–25 s |
| opens with a conjunction or sums up | **drop-cap** stack | ≤ 1 per 15–25 s |
| lists things, brands, services, places (2–4 items) | **icon tiles**, one per item, each on its word | cluster above the head; ≥ 2 items, else stay with type |
| names pieces of content (videos, posts, works, products) or their numbers (views, sales) | **card fan** with counters | the counter may animate to the spoken number |
| names a tool, app or program | the tool's **icon** on a glass tile; may **turn into a button** that leads into an app scene | real logo only with permission |
| describes a routine, a to-do, steps, effort ("you spend hours on…") | **app scene: to-do list** | items add and tick one by one |
| describes excuses, messages, what someone says or writes | **app scene: chat** | bubbles arrive one by one |
| a decision or a call to act ("just press…", "start…") | **button** (pill with "+" or a verb) | pressed on the word |
| change of camera set-up | **negative stutter** | every set-up cut |
| a punch word inside one set-up, at most twice per film | **bloom glitch** | 5–6 frames, on the word |

## Densities (per seconds of speech)

- Stacks: continuous; one phrase stack every 1.5–3.5 s.
- Accent key word: exactly one per stack.
- Figure bar: only where a figure is spoken.
- Headline + drop-cap together: at most one per 8–12 s.
- Icon tiles / card fan: at most one cluster per 6–10 s.
- App scenes: at most one per 10–15 s, each 1.5–4.5 s long; never two in a row without the speaker between them, except a light scene that dims into a dark one (one idea in two steps).
- Bloom glitch: at most 2 per film.

## Structure

- First 2 s: a stack must be building by the first spoken word; a device or a strong accent word in the first 3 s.
- The speaker is on screen at least 60 % of the time.
- App scenes are inserts. They end with a cut, or a stutter back to the speaker.
- Last 2 s: the speaker on screen, live, with the last stack or an end line building; never a frozen frame.

## Plan table
