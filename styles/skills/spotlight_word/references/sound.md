# Sound

## Layers

1. **Speech** — untouched timing, light cleanup. It carries the film; the style works without music.
2. **Optional bed** — soft, minimal instrumental (piano/ambient/light pulse), ducked 15–18 dB under speech. Leave it out if the speaker's room tone is the mood.
3. **Hits** — a short low hit (60–120 Hz, 150–300 ms) on the big devices only: split-layout entry, place reveal, pattern rise.
4. **Small sounds** — very soft pops for spotlight words (not every one; the first of each sentence at most), a short scribble/pen sound under script write-ons, soft clicks under tile transitions.

## Rules

- No sound on body words.
- Hits never on two consecutive devices closer than 2 s.
- Pull any bed down to silence for the last word if the film ends on a spotlight.

## Levels

- Speech ≈ −16 LUFS short-term; bed −30…−33 LUFS; hits ~2 dB above the bed.
- Master −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after the final encode. Measure (`ebur128=peak=true`) and report.
