# Beat map: moment → device

Anchor every device to the word that triggers it (audio onset). Most beats are interview + one-word captions; the devices below mark the moments that carry the story.

## Device table

| The speech does | Device | Anchor | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Says the speaker's name / role | **Spotlight** (+ lead words), optional stack swap for a second role | the name / role | the phrase |
| Names a brand / company / project | **Spotlight + script accent**; optionally a **pattern rise** behind the speaker (blocks in the spotlight colour forming a silhouette that fits the brand's field — skyline, shelves, waves…) | the brand name | 0.6–1.0 s |
| Talks about what they make / their work | **Full-frame B-roll of the work**, 1–1.5 s per piece, joined by **tile transitions** | the work noun / verb | 2–4 s, 2–4 pieces |
| Repeats or lands on a key name | **Type echo**: the spotlight word repeated as a column filling the frame | the word | 8–12 f, then cut |
| Names a place | **Place type**: a photo (or drawing) of the place, the name in huge vertical/stacked two-tone letters; entered by a **shape reveal** growing from the bottom of the interview shot | the place word | 1.5–2.5 s |
| Describes a journey / growth / steps ("from X to Y", "step by step", "all the way to") | **Metaphor split layout**: speaker in a top window, a monochrome metaphor illustration below (stairs, road, ladder, bridge, path), X small at the start, Y spotlighted at the end | the start word | the phrase (2–4 s) |
| Names a place inside the split layout | **Landmark illustration** rising from a bottom corner of the lower panel + place spotlight / script | the place | the phrase |
| Decisive verb / result | **Spotlight** on the verb, optional cut to a closer framing | the verb | the phrase |
| Plain talk | interview + one-word captions; re-frame cut on a phrase boundary | — | 2–5 s per shot |

## Density rules (any length)

| Element | Rate |
|---|---|
| Spotlight words | one per 1.5–3 s of speech |
| A device (B-roll run, place, split layout, echo, pattern rise) | one per 3–5 s of speech |
| Split layout | ≤ 1 per 20 s; each 2–4 s |
| Type echo | ≤ 1 per 15 s, ≤ 2 per film |
| Script accents | ≤ 1 per sentence |
| Interview stretch without a device | ≤ 6 s |

- Never two full-frame devices back to back without the speaker between, except work B-roll pieces joined by tiles.
- Short clips (< 20 s): name + brand spotlights, one work run, one place or split layout.
