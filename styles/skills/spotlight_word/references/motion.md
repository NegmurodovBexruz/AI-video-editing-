# Motion: entries, exits, frame counts (30 fps)

## Captions

| Element | Entry | Hold | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body word | appears on its onset, 2 f fade/scale 0.94 → 1.0 | until the next word | hard cut |
| Spotlight word | 3–4 f: scale 0.85 → 1.04 → 1.0, glow rises from 0 to full over 4–6 f | until the sentence moves on | hard cut (or pushed out by a stack swap) |
| Lead words above a spotlight | appear one by one on their onsets | while the spotlight holds | with the spotlight |
| Stack swap | new spotlight drops in above, old one pushed down and out, 6–8 f ease-out | — | — |
| Script accent | written on stroke by stroke, 10–15 f | with its spotlight | with its spotlight |

## Transitions

| Transition | Use | Frames |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | between interview framings, out of a device | 0 |
| Window shrink / grow | into / out of the metaphor split layout | 8–10 f |
| Shape reveal | interview → place photo | 6–8 f |
| Tile / block | between pieces of work | 10–15 f |
| Type echo | punctuation before a cut | 8–12 f |
| Pattern rise + pixel dissolve | brand moment | 10–14 f + 6–8 f |

No whip pans, spins, glitches, light leaks or white flashes.

## Camera

- Re-frame cuts (wide ↔ close) on phrase boundaries; optional slow push on long holds (1.00 → 1.04 over 3 s). No shake.

## Pace check

Count visual events per 10 s (cut, device, spotlight). Target: 3–5 spotlights and 2–3 devices per 10 s.
