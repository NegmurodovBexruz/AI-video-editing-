# Devices: build specs

`W`/`H` = frame width/height, `f` = frames at 30 fps, px at 1080 wide (scale with the frame). `SPOT` = spotlight colour (default yellow `#FFE600`).

## Metaphor split layout

- **Entry:** the interview frame shrinks (8–10 f, ease-in-out) into a rounded window at the top: ≈ 0.86 W × 0.36 H, centred, top margin ≈ 0.04 H, corner radius ≈ 0.04 W. The rest becomes a dark panel (`#161616`).
- **Illustration:** a monochrome (white/grey on dark) drawing or render of the metaphor fills the lower ≈ 55 % — stairs or a spiral staircase for climbing, a road or path for a journey, a ladder, a bridge for connecting, a bar chart only if the speaker gives numbers. It enters by rising from the bottom (8–12 f) or drawing on.
- **Labels:** the start word (X) small and grey at the start of the metaphor (bottom of the stairs, start of the road); the end word (Y) as a spotlight near the end of the metaphor when it is said; the lead words stack above it.
- **Exit:** the window grows back to full frame (8 f) or hard cut.

## Landmark illustration

- A monochrome illustration of a place's best-known landmark rising from a bottom corner of the lower panel (or of the full frame) over 8–12 f; ≈ 0.35–0.5 H tall; partly cut by the frame edge.
- With the place spotlight and an optional script accent (pattern: small lead word, spotlight place, script word — e.g. "old / Lisbon / district").

## Place type over a photo

- **Entry — shape reveal:** a tall shape that echoes the place's silhouette (a spire, a tower, an arch) or a simple rounded bar grows up from the bottom of the interview shot (6–8 f) and opens into the photo.
- The place photo (the user's, or a clearly illustrative image) full frame, slow push 1.00 → 1.04.
- The place name in **huge letters stacked vertically** (one letter per line) along one edge, height ≈ 0.9 H, bold, **two-tone**: split horizontally into two colours sampled from the photo or the brand (e.g. a cool top and a warm bottom); letters may sit partly behind the photo's main subject when a matte is available.

## Tile / block transition (between pieces of work)

- The next image appears through a grid of rectangles (8–16 tiles of different sizes) that flip/slide in, staggered over 10–15 f, sometimes revealing an intermediate state (a sketch before the render, an outline before the product).
- Use only between related pieces; never into or out of the interview.

## Type echo

- The spotlight word repeated in a column (6–10 rows) filling the frame, SPOT on top of the current shot, rows slightly offset, for 8–12 f; the centre row stays white or brighter. Hard cut out.

## Pattern rise (brand)

- Small square blocks in SPOT rise from the bottom behind the speaker (with a matte; in front at the frame bottom without one) forming a silhouette that fits the brand's field (skyline for building, shelves for retail, waves for audio, grid for tech) over 10–14 f, then dissolve into pixels over 6–8 f.

## Work B-roll

- The speaker's own work full frame, 1–1.5 s per piece, each with a slow push; one-word captions continue on top.
- Generated stills only when the user has no media, and labelled as illustrative in the README.
