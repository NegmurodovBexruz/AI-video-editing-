# Sound

## Layers

1. **Speech** — untouched timing, light cleanup (de-noise, de-ess, gentle compression). It carries the film.
2. **Optional bed** — light instrumental (soft pulse, minimal piano, lo-fi), ducked 15–18 dB under speech. The style works without one.
3. **Transition sounds** — a short plastic crinkle/rustle under every cellophane wipe; a soft airy shimmer or reverse swell (≤ 0.4 s) into a glow-silhouette flash.
4. **Small accents** — a very soft whoosh (≤ 0.25 s) under zoom-out reveals and the biggest punches only; a soft low thump (60–120 Hz) on at most one back word per 15 s; a short pen scribble under script write-ons.

## Rules

- No sound on small words, sub-lines or ordinary punches.
- Never two accent sounds closer than 1.5 s.
- Pull any bed down for the last sentence if the film ends on a statement or a call to action.

## Levels

- Speech ≈ −16 LUFS short-term; bed −30…−33 LUFS; transition sounds ~3 dB above the bed, below speech.
- Master −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP **after** the final encode (encode at −1.5 or lower; AAC adds peak). Measure (`ebur128=peak=true`) and report.
