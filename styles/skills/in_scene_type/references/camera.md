# Camera: zoom pulses, reveals, pushes (digital, 30 fps)

All moves are digital crops of the source, centred on the head (keep the eyes at 0.28–0.38 H after the move). Scale values are relative to the framing before the move.

| Move | Size | Frames | Easing | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Punch** | +4 … +16 % | 4–8 f | ease-out (largest step first) | optional small rise (0.01–0.03 H), tilt or roll ≤ 1.5° in the same direction for the whole move |
| **Hold** | — | 0.4–1.5 s | — | the frame stays still; no drift |
| **Release** | −6 … −10 % (back to, or near, the framing before) | 4–9 f | ease-out or linear | undo the roll |
| **Zoom-out reveal** | −18 … −25 % | 11–14 f | ease-in-out | may pan to re-centre the speaker; the big word lands at the end of it |
| **Accelerating push** | +25 … +45 % over the shot | 3–5 s | ease-in (speed grows ≈ 4×) | ends with the cut / transition, never held |
| **Opening pulse** | out −15 … −20 % (8–11 f) on the first word, then punch in on the hook word | — | ease-in-out / ease-out | only at the start of the film |
| **Reframe jump** | hard cut to a ±8–15 % different crop | 0 f | — | inside a set-up, on a phrase boundary, to hide a jump in the take |

## Rules

- **Punch → hold → release** is the basic unit. Do not stack punches past +25 % total without a release.
- A punch lands **on the onset of the emphasised word** (its first frame of motion 0–2 f after the onset).
- A release often coincides with a back word appearing: the person gets smaller as the word arrives behind them.
- A zoom-out reveal ends on the onset of the big word it makes room for.
- Keep digital scale ≤ 1.5× of a 4K source, ≤ 1.25× of a 1080 source (more looks soft); plan punches accordingly.
- Motion blur on punches and reveals: a light directional blur (or 180° shutter simulation) during moves of more than 1.5 % per frame.
- No camera shake, no whip pans, no spins.
- The final set-up of the film: no moves (or one gentle push ≤ 5 %).
