# Look: palette, fonts, grade

## Palette

| Role | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main type | white `#FFFFFF` | back, front, chest words, figures, script |
| Grey back word | white at 40–60 % or grey `#8A8A8A` at 70 % | reads as part of the wall |
| Small words | white, or light grey `#D9D9D9` before turning white | clusters |
| Accent (`ACC`) | warm amber, e.g. `#E8A33C` | sub-lines only; replace with the brand colour if there is one |
| Shadows | black 30–40 %, blur 20 px | only where the background is bright; never boxes |

One accent colour per film. No gradients on type, no outlines.

## Fonts (free / OFL defaults — swap for brand fonts)

| Role | Default | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Huge key words | Anton | Bebas Neue, Oswald Bold, League Gothic |
| Chest words, clusters, sub-lines | Inter (Bold for chest words, Regular for small words) | Manrope, Montserrat |
| Figures | Playfair Display Italic (light/regular) | Cormorant Garamond Italic, DM Serif Display Italic |
| Script | Caveat | Homemade Apple, Dancing Script |

Check every font has the language's letters (e.g. oʻ, gʻ, ş, ı, ñ, Cyrillic) before planning; substitute a font with full coverage rather than fake glyphs.

## Grade

- Natural, clean, slightly warm skin; keep each set-up's own colour (rooms may differ).
- No heavy look, no grain, no vignette beyond a very light one (≤ 10 %).
- Type must read on bright walls: when a back word is white on a light wall, darken the wall behind it slightly (local −0.2…−0.4 EV) or switch to the front layer.

## Brand override

Brand colour → `ACC`. Brand fonts → replace the matching role. Keep the role split (condensed caps / sans / serif italic / script).
