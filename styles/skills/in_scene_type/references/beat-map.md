# Beat map: speech → composition, camera, device

Anchor everything to the word that triggers it (its audio onset). Most beats are a phrase composition on the talking shot; camera moves and devices mark the moments that carry the message.

## Device table

| The speech does | Type | Camera | Device |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opens the video with a hook | key word as a chest or front word, cluster beside the head | zoom-out on the first word, then a punch on the hook's key word | — |
| States a figure (number, %, amount) | figure (thin serif italic, written on) or chest word | punch on the figure | if the speaker shows it with fingers or a gesture: **gesture spawn** |
| Makes the main claim / contrast of a passage | **back word** (behind the head) | punch → hold → release; or release exactly when the back word appears | — |
| Names the thing the passage is about | **front word** across the chest | **zoom-out reveal** that makes room for it | — |
| Evaluates ("unfair", "easy", "ignored") | script word written on | — | — |
| Names an organisation with a known mark | — | small reframe | **on-body logo** (with permission) |
| Ordinary phrase | cluster (+ chest word if there is a key word) | none, or a slow drift | — |
| Changes topic / set-up | — | — | **cellophane wipe** or **glow-silhouette flash** at the set-up change; hard cut otherwise |
| Long phrase on one set-up (> 5 s) | — | slow accelerating push that ends at the cut | — |
| Final sentence / call to action | chest word or figure | static camera | none |

## Density rules (any length)

| Element | Rate |
|---|---|
| Phrase composition | every phrase |
| Back or front word | one per 5–8 s of speech |
| Camera move (punch, release, reveal) | one per 2–4 s of speech in talking stretches |
| Script words | ≤ 1 per 15 s |
| Gesture spawn | only when the gesture really shows the figure/thing |
| Physical transition | only at a set-up change; ≤ 1 per 8 s; never twice in a row of the same kind within 10 s unless the film has only one kind |
| Static stretch (no camera move) | ≤ 5 s, except the final set-up |

- Never a back word and a front word in the same composition.
- Never a punch while a gesture spawn is entering (the number must land on a still frame).
- Short clips (< 20 s): one back or front word, one reveal, two or three punches, one transition at most.
