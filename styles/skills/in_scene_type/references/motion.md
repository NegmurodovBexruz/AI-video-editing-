# Motion: entries, exits, frame counts (30 fps)

## Type

| Element | Entry | Hold | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back word (blur-settle) | opacity 0 → 100 % in 4 f; blur 18–28 → 0 px and scale 1.08 → 1.00 in 6–12 f, ease-out | screen-fixed until the phrase ends | blur → 16 px + fade, 4–6 f |
| Back word (slide) | slides 0.15–0.30 W out from behind the head, 8–12 f, ease-out | screen-fixed | fade 4–6 f |
| Front word | blur-settle as above | screen-fixed | blur + fade 4–6 f |
| Chest word | blur 10–14 → 0 px, opacity 0 → 100 %, 4–6 f | head-locked if beside the head, else screen-fixed | fade 4 f |
| Figure | left-to-right soft wipe, 8–14 f | screen-fixed | fade 4–6 f |
| Cluster word | opacity 0 → 100 %, blur 6 → 0 px, 3–4 f (or grey → white, 4 f) | head-locked | all lines together, blur + fade 4–6 f |
| Sub-line word | fade 2–3 f | follows its key word | with its key word |
| Gesture spawn | blur 20 → 0 px + opacity, 5–7 f, at the hand | screen-fixed | fade 4–6 f |
| Script word | stroke write-on 10–15 f | glued to the surface | fade 4–6 f |

- No bounces, no overshoot on opacity, no letter-by-letter typewriter on small words.
- Entries are soft (blur and opacity), exits are quicker than entries.

## Transitions

| Transition | Use | Frames |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | between phrases on a reframe; between set-ups when no physical transition is planned | 0 |
| Cellophane wipe | set-up change | 4–7 f, cut under the densest frame |
| Glow-silhouette flash | set-up change on a turn in the story | 3–5 f |
| Reframe jump | inside a set-up | 0 |

No light leaks, glitches, flashes of white over the whole frame, spins or whip pans.

## Pace check

Count visual events per 10 s (camera move, big word, transition). Target: 3–5 camera moves, 1–2 big words (back/front/figure), ≤ 1 transition per 10 s.
