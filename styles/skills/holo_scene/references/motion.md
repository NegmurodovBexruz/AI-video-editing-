# Motion: entries, exits, transitions, camera (30 fps)

## Type

| Element | Entry | Hold | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key word | letters left → right with a soft edge, 7–8 f; blur 6 → 0 px | screen-fixed | fade + blur 5 f (2 f before the next stack) |
| Lead line / lines | per word on its onset: opacity 3–4 f, grey → white 4 f, blur 2 → 0 | screen-fixed | with the stack |
| Counting key word | counts with its device (ease-out), lands on the spoken figure | — | with the stack |
| Labels in the dive | scale 0.35 → 2.5 in 12–13 f, outward to a wall | — | fade in the last 20 % |
| Tracked label on a cutaway | ease-out 9 f | follows the object | with the shot |

## Transitions

| Transition | Where | Frames |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | between set-ups, in and out of product beats and cutaways | 0 |
| Into the dive | hard cut on the phrase start, the clip's motion already running | 0 |
| Out of the dive | white end → next shot rises out of white | 6 |
| Into the end card | white flash | 6 |

No dissolves, glitches, whip pans, light leaks or spins.

## Camera (digital, on the real shots)

- A slow push over a set-up (1.00 → 1.05–1.06), linear or ease-in-out. Stay within the sharp limit of the source (≤ 1.33× of a 1440p+ working copy).
- One punch per film on the question or the main turn: +8–10 % in 6 f (ease-out), held to the cut.
- Hologram positions, matte and tracking use the **same** zoom transform as the footage.

## Pace check

Count events per 10 s (cut, device entry, interlude, product beat). Target 3–5. One device active at a time on a surface.
