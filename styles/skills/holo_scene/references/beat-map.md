# Beat map: claim → device → surface

Anchor every device to the word that triggers it (its audio onset). First decide **where in the real shot** the device lives: the surface. Then decide what it shows.

## Surfaces (find them on the placement sheet)

| Surface | Typical place | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hands** | between open palms, above clasped hands (below the chin) | the named object, small and precious; a part that is then revealed |
| **Wall of repeated objects** | shelves, stacks, rows, windows, seats behind the person | quantities, scale, "all of them", a wave that counts |
| **Floor / table** | free floor beside the person, a table in front | something that *grows*: a model, a map, a building, a grid of many items |
| **Above the head** | sky, ceiling, a wall above the person | a screen, a sign, a hanging object |
| **Hook / anchor point** | a hook, a beam, a shelf edge, a hand-held item | an object that hangs or is held |

Never place VFX on the face; keep ≥ 0.15 FW from it. In front of the body, keep below the chin.

## Device table

| The speech does | Device | Surface | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opens with a scene or a famous thing | **Hologram object** appears with a scan, sways or turns | hook, floor, beside the person | slow push 1.00 → 1.06 over the set-up |
| States a quantity of something present | **Row lighting** or **wave and counter** on the repeated objects | wall | static |
| Names a thing and its parts / material | **Object in the hands**, then **skin → frame reveal** | hands | static |
| Turns one thing into many / into another | **Sparks into place**: the hologram breaks into particles that fly into the real objects, which then glow | hands → wall | static |
| Gives a dimension or a spec | **Section drawing**: rings or lines drawn on, with dimension marks | between the hands | slow push |
| Gives a length, distance or a "how far" | **The dive** (CGI interlude) with marks on the walls, ending white | — (full frame) | the dive itself |
| Compares with something famous | **Cutaway** (generated clip) with a tracked label on the compared thing | — (full frame) | the clip's own move |
| Compares sizes or counts in the place | **Grow from the surface**: a model rises from the floor/table; many small copies drop in rows | floor / table | slow push, punch on the question |
| Explains the product or service | **Full-screen product** beat (site/app/screen recording) with a lower caption | — (full frame) | slow scroll |
| Calls to action | **Screen assembles** from particles, a cursor uses it | above the head / behind | static or gentle push |
| Ends | **Moving end card** | — | — |

## Densities (any length)

| Element | Rate |
|---|---|
| Caption stack | every phrase |
| In-scene VFX | ≥ 1 per set-up; one device active at a time; a new device every 3–6 s of speech |
| The dive (CGI interlude) | exactly one per film (≤ 6 s; in films > 90 s, one more is allowed) |
| Generated cutaways | ≤ 2 per 30 s, each 2–4 s |
| Full-screen product beats | 0–2, each 1–4.5 s |
| Assembling screen | one, near the end |
| Footage share | ≥ 70 % of the runtime shows the real location |
| Set-up changes | hard cuts, about every 3–6 s; the dive and the white flash are the only transitions |

- Never two in-scene devices on the same surface at the same time. A device leaves before the next one enters on that surface.
- Short clips (< 25 s): one hologram object, one quantity device, the dive *or* a cutaway, the screen, the end card.

## Story shapes (pick the one the speech already has)

- **Case:** everyday scene → the task → what was built → 2–3 mechanisms (one device each) → result → CTA → mark.
- **Fact explainer:** famous thing → its numbers → transformation into the brand's product → scale (the dive) → comparison → question → next episode / CTA → mark.
- **Product:** problem → the product appears (in the hands) → how it works (devices on surfaces) → where it is (screen) → CTA → mark.
