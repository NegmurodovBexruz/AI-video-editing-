# Motion: transitions and frame counts (30 fps)

## Caption words

Reveal in place (grey/soft → white, 3–4 f), no movement — see `captions.md`. The caption block never moves.

## Transitions

| Transition | Use | Frames |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | speaker re-frames, back from any stage section, between portfolio cards | 0 |
| Iris-to-dot | speaker → branching diagram | 8–10 f circle shrink (soft white rim) + 4–6 f to a dot; line grows 6–8 f |
| Glitch-smear | speaker → concept badge (or any stage entry that should feel energetic) | 1 f brand-colour tint over the footage → 2–3 f horizontal smear/displacement with blocky remnants → 4–6 f dissolve to the stage |
| Fast slide + motion blur | into an item colour card | 4–6 f |
| Scale-in | portfolio card on the stage | 10–12 f, 0.25 → 1.0, ease-out |
| Particle disintegration | on a change verb, over a still/B-roll | 45–75 f |
| Brand-mark sting | open / close | open ≤ 20 f; close 45–60 f ending on the static mark |

No whip pans, spins, cube or page turns, no light leaks, no white flashes.

## Element motion

| Element | Entry | Hold | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Item card label | typed on, 2 f per character | static | with the card |
| Diagram camera | — | ease-in-out 0.6–0.8 s per item | cut |
| Edge tile | slide from the nearest edge, 6–8 f ease-out | static | cut with the chunk or slide out 6 f |
| Concept logotype | drop from above 12–15 f ease-out | slow drift ≤ 2 % | blur-out 6–8 f |
| Badge rings | expand 0.2 W → 0.6 W over ~30 f, staggered 6–8 f, fading | — | — |
| Portfolio card | scale-in 10–12 f | push 1.00 → 1.05 | hard cut |
| B-roll still | — | push 1.00 → 1.06 | hard cut |

## Pace check

Count visual events per 10 s (cut, re-frame, stage entry, card, B-roll, sting). Target 3–5. The speaker sections are calm; energy comes from the stage moments.
