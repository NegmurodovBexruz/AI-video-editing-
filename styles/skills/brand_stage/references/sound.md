# Sound

## Layers

1. **Speech** — untouched timing; light cleanup.
2. **Music bed** — modern instrumental (minimal electronic / ambient tech pulse), steady, under everything, ducked 12–15 dB below speech. May lift slightly on stage sections.
3. **Stage hits** — a short low hit or sub thump (60–120 Hz, 150–300 ms) on each stage entry, badge landing and portfolio sequence start. Not on every cut.
4. **Sting sound** — a short designed swell + hit with the brand-mark open and close (≤ 0.7 s open; the close may ring out under the last words).
5. **Small UI sounds** — very soft ticks for typed labels and UI-tag boxes, soft pops for edge tiles; a fine granular crackle under particle disintegration.

## Rules

- Hits mark stage moments only; speaker re-frames stay silent apart from the bed.
- Keep transitions subtle: a short airy swish under iris-to-dot and glitch-smear is allowed but must sit below the hit and the voice.
- Music ends with the film on a phrase boundary or a short tail under the end mark.

## Levels

- Speech ≈ −16 LUFS short-term, music −28…−31 LUFS under speech, hits ~2 dB above the bed.
- Master −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (check after the final AAC encode). Measure (`ebur128=peak=true`) and report the numbers.

## Licences

- Use only music/SFX the user may publish. Free-tier generator outputs usually carry no commercial licence; check the tier first (HTTP 402 / "paid plan required" is the tell). Synthesised SFX are always safe. Never download copyrighted tracks.
