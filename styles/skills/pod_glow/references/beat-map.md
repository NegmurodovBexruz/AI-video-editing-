# Beat map: idea → device

Work from word timings. A **beat** is one idea (one sentence or clause, 2–6 s). A beat gets at most one *card*; most beats get captions only. Anchor a card's entry to the first word of the phrase that names the idea (≈ 0.1–0.5 s before the concept noun), and end it on a phrase boundary or a cut.

## Device table

| The speech contains | Device | Anchor | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| The **central concept** of the clip (a noun that the whole clip is about: community, trust, burnout, growth…) | **Capsule → orbit**: footage shrinks into a rounded capsule window, the view pulls back into dark space with concentric accent orbit rings; 3–6 tag pills orbit; the concept as a two-tier caption under the capsule | first word of the concept phrase | 1.5–2.0 s, once per clip (≤ 1 per 60 s) |
| People / a shared experience ("you and I", "we both", "people like us") | **White card + photo**: white background, a tilted photo card with long soft shadow, small accent italic line above, big condensed words revealed *behind* the photo | the pronoun pair / subject | 1.0–1.4 s |
| **Connection / reaching others** (connect, network, meet, find someone) | **Network**: 3–5 accent person-icons pop around the speaker, linked by glowing accent lines that draw in | the verb | until the next cut / 1.2–1.8 s |
| **Working together / exchange** (collaborate, partner, swap, team up) | **White card + two tiles**: chrome/silver condensed title, two tilted 3D tiles (speaker still ↔ generic avatar) with a swap icon between, accent swoosh behind | the verb | 2.0–2.5 s |
| A **named term / idiom / concept with a definition** ("<term> is when…", "there's this thing called…") | **Glass lower card**: dark translucent rounded panel, white bold title, accent rule, typewriter sub-line with the explanation | the term | the whole definition (1.5–2.5 s) |
| A **question** the speaker asks | **Hollow "???"** behind the key phrase | the question word | the question |
| An **emotion word** (frustrating, scary, love) | **Emoji attachment** on the key phrase (no card) | the word | the chunk |
| A **concrete quantity** (years, people, money) | **Icon badge** on the key phrase; unit small below | the number | the chunk |
| A **group of people** named ("creators", "founders", "parents") | **Avatar row** pill under the key phrase | the group noun | the chunk |
| A **turn in the conversation** (new section, "and I think…", the other guest answers) | **Accent light leak** washing across the shot | the first word of the new section | 0.4–0.6 s |
| Speaker change | **White flash cut** | sentence boundary | 2 frames |
| Plain talk | **Breathing**: speaker + captions; re-frame wide ↔ tight on phrase boundaries | — | ≥ 2 s after each card |
| The closing line | **Word-by-word key phrase build** over live footage | each word | to the end |

## Density rules (any length)

Rates are per amount of speech, so they hold for a 20-second clip and a 3-minute interview alike.

| Element | Rate |
|---|---|
| Visual event (cut, re-frame, card, flash, leak, caption shape change) | every 2–3 s; no single framing longer than ~4 s |
| Card (any device that is more than a caption) | one per 5–8 s of speech on average |
| Full-screen card (orbit, white cards) | ≤ 1 per 12 s; each ≤ 2.5 s |
| Capsule → orbit | once per clip, for its central concept; ≤ 1 per 60 s in long clips |
| Emoji | ≤ 1 per 8 s |
| Light leak | one per section turn, ≤ 1 per 20 s |
| White flash | every speaker change, nowhere else except a white-card exit |
| Breathing (speaker + captions only) | ≥ 2 s after each card |

- Never two full-screen cards back to back; the speaker returns between them.
- Cards that hide the speaker are short (≤ 2.5 s); overlays on the speaker (network, glass card, ???) may run with their phrase.
- A single-speaker clip has no speaker-switch flashes; carry its rhythm with re-frames, cards and leaks.
- Long clips: keep the rates, vary the devices (don't repeat the same card type twice in a row), and let section turns (new question, new topic) set where leaks go.
