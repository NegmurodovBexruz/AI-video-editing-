# Devices: build specs

`W`/`H` = frame width/height, `f` = frames at 30 fps, px at 1080 wide (scale with the frame). `ACCENT` = the agency accent (default in `look.md`).

## Slot-roll counter in a capsule (hook promise)

- Rounded capsule outline (2–3 px white, 60–70 % opacity, radius = half height) ≈ 0.42 W × 0.13 W, centred at ≈ 42–48 % of the height.
- Inside: the number in Bold, ≈ 0.11–0.13 W tall; a small unit word under it inside the capsule (caps, ≈ 22–26 px, tracked +8 %) — e.g. the magnitude word ("MILLION", "K") when the number is abbreviated.
- **Roll:** each digit column scrolls vertically through 6–10 random digits with vertical motion blur and lands on its value; columns land left to right, staggered 2–3 f; total 15–20 f. The decimal point/comma is static.
- Metric word below the capsule types in (2 f per character) on its word.
- **Falling icons (optional, only with an obvious icon for the metric):** 8–16 small 3D-style objects drop from above the capsule with gravity, bounce once and pile at ≈ 60 % of the height over 1–1.5 s. Never use money for a non-money metric.
- Number shown = the fact sheet value, abbreviated the way it is spoken ("two and a half million" → "2.5 M").

## Counter + progress bar

- Big rolling number (same roll) at the lower-left third (left margin ≈ 0.08 W, ≈ 60–65 % of the height), ≈ 0.11 W tall.
- A thin bar (4–6 px) in ACCENT under it, growing left → right from the frame edge to under the number's end over 15–20 f; a tiny caps label under the bar's end ("CLICKS").

## Partner badge

- Rounded badge (radius ≈ 0.35 × height), height ≈ 0.085 W, filled with the partner's colour (or a neutral dark with white text if no permission for their colours/mark), the partner's wordmark or name in it.
- Pops in on the name: scale 0.6 → 1.05 → 1.0 over 6 f; a second badge stacks under the first with a 0.02 W gap. Replaces the caption for that chunk; holds until the next chunk.

## Strobe montage

- 12–16 images (the real ads/creatives/variants), **2 frames each**, full frame, cover fit; the body caption (the trigger word) holds over the whole montage.
- Order for contrast: alternate light/dark and product/people shots; end on the strongest piece for 4–6 f before the cut back.

## Phone / UI frame overlay

- A minimal vertical-video UI mock (thin white status/side icons, a progress line) framing the presenter for 0.8–1.5 s, with a 3–4 f fade; may include one guide label in tiny caps. Never use a real app's trademarked UI unless permitted — keep it generic.

## Client B-roll

- The client's own product/lifestyle media, full frame, hard cut in/out or entering through a soft warm flare (6–10 f). Keep the client's own grade; their mark may stay where it already is in the media, or appear small and centred for the first shot only.
- Body captions continue on top — check contrast on every shot (`captions.md` → *Readability*).

## Film-burn light leak + tinted re-entry

- Warm burn (red → orange → pink blooms, irregular blobs, Add/Screen) rising over the outgoing shot for 8–12 f, peaking near white.
- The incoming shot starts with a cross-processed tint (green/teal for a warm source, warm for a cool one) at 50–70 % and settles to the normal grade over 4–6 f.

## Colour leak (section turn, softer)

- A pink/magenta (or accent) soft leak sweeping from one side over 8–12 f, peak 40–60 %, no tint after.

## Agency mark at the CTA

- The agency's mark (from the user) pops in under the caption when the agency is named: from a slight offset (≈ 0.05 W) and 0.9 scale to centre and 1.0 over 6–8 f; holds 0.8–1.2 s; leaves with the chunk.
- The last shot is live footage; no frozen frame, no separate end card unless the user supplies one.
