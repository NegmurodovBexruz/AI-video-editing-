# Sound

## Layers

1. **Speech** — untouched timing; light cleanup; match levels if the hook was recorded elsewhere.
2. **Music bed** — modern, confident, mid-tempo (95–120 BPM), instrumental, under everything, ducked 12–15 dB below speech; may change energy at the result section.
3. **Counter sounds** — fast soft ticks while digits roll, a short "land" hit when the number settles; a soft whoosh-free rise under a filling progress bar.
4. **Falling icons** — light paper/coin/pop impacts matched to the icon, low in the mix.
5. **Montage pulse** — a short rhythmic pulse or shutter-like ticks under the strobe (one per 2–4 images, not per image).
6. **Leaks and badges** — a soft swell under film-burn leaks; small pops for partner badges and the agency mark.

## Rules

- Every counter landing has a sound; plain cuts don't.
- Pull the music back slightly (−2 dB) under the result numbers so they land clearly.
- Music ends on the CTA phrase boundary or with a short tail under the agency mark.

## Levels

- Speech ≈ −16 LUFS short-term, music −28…−31 LUFS under speech, effects ~2 dB above the bed.
- Master −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after the final encode. Measure (`ebur128=peak=true`) and report.
