# Story structure: the case-study template

Split the speech into these sections in order. Shares are of the total runtime and are guides, not quotas; a section the speaker skips stays skipped (don't invent content to fill it).

| # | Section | Job | Typical share | Speech markers (any language, paraphrase) | Screen marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | Promise the result with a number | 8–14 % (≤ 7 s) | "here's how…", "this made…", a number + a period | boldest kinetic type, counters; may be a different location or a brighter grade |
| 2 | **Client** | Who it was for and what they already had | 5–8 % | the client's name, "already had…" | client media with the client's mark |
| 3 | **Challenge** | What was hard / missing | 6–9 % | "the challenge / problem was…", "but…" | headline caption; back to the main location |
| 4 | **Strategy** | What was done (a list) | 15–22 % | "so we…", "we created / set up / ran…", list items | presenter + one B-roll or badge per item |
| 5 | **Test** | How it was refined | 7–12 % | "we tested…", "different X, Y, Z" | strobe montage of the variants |
| 6 | **Result** | The numbers | 12–20 % | "the result?", numbers, "over", "nearly" | headline caption + counters/bars on each number |
| 7 | **Lesson** | Why it worked, in one idea | 8–14 % | "because…", "it's not about… it's about…" | B-roll, then presenter; a light leak into it |
| 8 | **Call to action** | What the viewer should do | 8–14 % | "if you…", "message us", "book…" | light leak at the turn; agency mark on its name at the end |

## Rules

- **Section changes are visible.** Mark each change with one of: a headline caption, a location/grade change, a light leak, a montage or B-roll entry. Never two markers stacked on one change.
- **The hook is self-contained.** It must make sense muted: the promise number is on screen as a counter, the metric word under it, the period ("in one month") as a caption.
- **Result before lesson.** If the speaker gives the lesson first, keep the order spoken (never retime speech) but put the strongest number device on the result section.
- **Long sections:** split the strategy or result into items; each item gets its own device so no stretch of the presenter runs longer than ~8 s without a change.
- **Short clips (< 25 s):** hook → result → CTA may be all there is; keep the hook counter, one result counter and the CTA mark.

## Plan table format (gate 1)

| # | Time | Section | Speech | Device | Source | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5 | Hook | "…over two million views…" | slot-roll counter "2 M" + metric word | user's fact sheet | 0 |

End with: credits, the fact table (spoken number → on-screen number → source), permissions needed, uncertain names.
