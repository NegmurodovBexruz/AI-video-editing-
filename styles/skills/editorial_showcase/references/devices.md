# Devices

Read only the selected sections. Geometry is relative to `W`/`H`; transition timings live in `motion.md`. None of these devices requires generative media by default.

## Message card

Use when someone requests, asks or gives feedback. Rounded neutral card roughly 0.55–0.75 W wide, with a short role label and one/two readable text lines. Place near an open hand only if it stays clear of the gesture; otherwise use a free corner above the caption zone. Enter with a small scale/opacity rise; no repeated bouncing.

Use supplied wording or an explicit paraphrase label. Do not fabricate a real app screenshot, timestamp or sender identity. A neutral notification design is enough; icons are optional. Hold long enough to read before leaving.

## Definition card

Full light/dark neutral frame with editorial type and one accent. Maximum 2–3 short lines, centred or aligned to a stable column within 0.80 W. Reveal the thought, not random character decoration. Keep the same two-family kit. Return to the presenter or to the evidence when the definition is complete.

## Presenter + lower demo

Reserve approximately the lower 0.28–0.40 H for a screen recording or document crop. Reframe the presenter into the remaining region without cutting the face or important hands. Allocate a caption strip/clear area explicitly; do not cover the demo's key action with subtitles.

Slide the panel up into its final area, then let the content move naturally. Avoid stretching a landscape website into a portrait panel. Capture the relevant region or use fit with a neutral surround. Readable interaction must be shown at a larger scale when this panel is too small.

## Three-row showcase

Three horizontal bands, equal height initially, filling the frame with a 0–0.008 H gutter. Each band shows a complementary view of the same subject: overview, use/detail, and result/another angle. Each uses its own deliberate source range and crop.

Rows may scroll or play independently, but their combined movement must stay readable. Use a soft local scrim for the supporting-caption zone if it overlaps media. If one row contains the key action, slow or hold the others. Do not animate three unrelated camera moves at once.

Use two bands or one full-screen view when there are fewer suitable sources. Label concept media as illustrative. Never fill a missing evidence slot with generated "client work". Fine UI detail gets a full-screen insert.

## Perspective panel handoff

Place outgoing and incoming compositions on two or three flat planes in a neutral field. Pull back just enough to reveal edges, yaw/translate the incoming panel into view, then push into it until it fills the output. Keep the source crop fixed inside each plane. This is 2.5D compositing, not a requirement for a generated 3D flight.

Text may travel with its panel, but cannot become a long unreadable skewed paragraph. Keep the transition brief and the final resting panel front-facing. No unrelated camera spin. On a source cut or short sentence, replace the handoff with a cut.

## Phone/screen container

The current composition can shrink into a neutral device outline, then the screen content switches to the supplied demo. Keep a real screen recording playable rather than treating it as a still. Remove unnecessary tiny UI details; add no fake product functionality. Use a plain screen instead of a phone when the content's aspect ratio demands it. One device is sufficient; do not nest phones inside phones.

## Document panorama

Use actual supplied pages, arranged as a column or compact grid with clear reading order. Move over page groups and stop at the important detail. At a fast page switch, a short zoom blur is optional. Do not ask the audience to read a full page while it flies by.

Without actual documents, draw an openly illustrative diagram with approved text. No fake citations, client documents or implied results.

## Editorial paper scene

Use an off-white page, a heading and quiet body texture. Move a virtual camera gently across it; change one high-contrast highlighted term on its spoken cue. Perspective should remain shallow enough for the highlighted word to read at phone size.

All readable background text must be supplied or approved copy. Omit it or use non-readable rules/texture when content is missing; never leave template filler. This is a short list/explanation scene, not a permanent texture over the whole video.

## Optional surface mark

A supplied mark can settle onto a visible table or other plane with a perspective transform and subtle contact shadow. Check clearance from hands. Track the plane if the camera moves; a static transform is only appropriate when the apparent plane remains stable. If tracking fails, use a flat wordmark or omit the device. Do not require this accent in every film.
