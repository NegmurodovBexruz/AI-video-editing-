# Sound

This style's sound defaults support the visual grammar; they are not a reconstruction of isolated tracks from a reference mix.

- Keep speech primary and continuous through demo and document cutaways. Clean/level it conservatively without flattening words or clipping consonants.
- Music is optional. Use supplied/licensed material when available; no missing music track requires paid generation. A quiet edit with sparse accents is a valid fallback.
- For a music bed, set gain by intelligibility, duck under speech with smooth attack/release and avoid audible pumping. Do not copy a numerical gain from an unrelated source recording.
- Use a quiet tick for a message or document change, a soft page/cloth sound for a paper move, and an optional low impact when a showcase lands. Do not put a sound on every word or every row.
- Do not add generic whooshes to routine panels or zooms. A specifically requested tunnel/dive is outside this style's standard transition set and follows the project's sound rule.
- Align each chosen effect to the visual event's actual landing/reveal. Leave breaths and pauses intact unless the brief asks for recutting.
- Master the final encoded file to −14 LUFS integrated ±0.5 and true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, unless the user provides another target. Measure after mux/export and check that the full intended video and speech tail remain present.
- Record sources and any synthesis/generation in the delivery notes. Never imply a music licence or an original SFX identity that has not been established.
